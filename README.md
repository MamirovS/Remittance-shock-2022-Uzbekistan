# Remittance Shocks and Household Welfare in Uzbekistan

LaTeX source for the master's thesis *Remittance Shocks and Household Welfare in Uzbekistan: Evidence from the 2022 Russia Crisis* (MSc Economics, University of Bonn, 2026).

The thesis uses the Listening to Citizens of Uzbekistan panel to ask whether the February 2022 invasion of Ukraine harmed households that depend on remittances from Russia.
